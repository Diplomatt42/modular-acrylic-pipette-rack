# Changelog

## v1.0

- Initial public release of the 3 mm shared-shelf modular acrylic rack.
- Solid-side and 2-inch corner-column variants; three unique cut parts each.
- Individual DXFs and STEP models; one-box and four-compartment assemblies.
- Editable parameters, laser profiles, source generator, fit coupons and layouts.
- Assembly instructions, AI handoff guidance, and checked geometry reports.
- CC BY 4.0 licensing with attribution and modification notices.
- Prototype release: physical fit, load capacity and stack stability untested.
