# Assembly reference: solid-side version

Coordinate system: X is shelf width; Y runs from the open front toward the back;
Z points upward. The bottom shelf underside is Z=0. The front is open and has
no retaining lip or incline.

1. Place one shelf flat on a level counter. Its twelve slots are the same on every
   shelf; front/back orientation matters. Use the STEP model to identify the rear
   crosswise slots, which are close to the back edge.
2. Add two full side panels and a back panel. Lower wall tabs enter the A slot set.
   Hold or temporarily brace the walls until the upper shelf caps them.
3. Place a second identical shelf on those walls. Upper wall tabs enter the B slot
   set; the A slots on this shared shelf are available for the next compartment.
4. Add the next set of walls onto the shared shelf and repeat. Four compartments
   finish with five shelves.
5. Check that the assembly is square, the base sits flat, and all tabs are seated.

The two side tabs at each joint zone have asymmetric positions: lower tabs are
at 31.25% of the 2-inch zone; upper tabs are at 68.75%. Back-panel upper and lower
tabs are offset as well. Do not reverse a wall vertically or use one slot set
for both opposing walls. The lower and upper tab outlines identify orientation.

Corner wall edges abut without vertical corner connectors. Shelves locate the
walls; no adhesive, fastener, or retainer is included. Current clearances are
deliberately loose and do not hold a rack together when lifted by its top. Move
it with support underneath or disassemble it. Acrylic press fits can crack and
are not obtained simply by setting clearance to zero.

Default tab projection is 2.0066 mm and model thickness is 2.9972 mm, leaving
0.9906 mm nominal clearance underneath the lower tabs inside the bottom shelf.
If your sheet is thinner than the projection, tabs will contact the counter.

There is no certified load rating or unlimited safe stack height. Changing the
height or compartment count requires reassessing sway, racking, and tipping.
